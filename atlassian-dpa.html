Data Processing Agreement

For Bizappe Apps on the Atlassian Marketplace

Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Effective Date: Jan 10, 2026

Overview

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement" or "DPA") forms part of the agreement between Bizappe ("Processor") and the customer entity ("Controller") that installs, accesses, or uses Bizappe's Atlassian Marketplace applications ("Services").

This Agreement governs the processing of Personal Data by Processor on behalf of Controller in connection with the Services.

1. Definitions

For purposes of this Agreement:

"Applicable Data Protection Laws" means all applicable laws governing the processing of Personal Data, including but not limited to:

  • Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)
  • UK GDPR
  • Applicable national data protection laws

"Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person processed by Processor on behalf of Controller.

"Processing" has the meaning given in Article 4(2) of the GDPR.

"Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by Processor to process Personal Data.

"Security Incident" means any unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration, or disclosure of Personal Data.

2. Scope of Processing

Processor shall process Personal Data solely:

  • To provide the Services
  • To fulfill obligations under the agreement with Controller
  • According to Controller's documented instructions

Processor shall not process Personal Data for its own purposes.

3. Nature and Purpose of Processing

The processing activities include:

  • Hosting and operating the Atlassian Marketplace application
  • Storing checklist/task/workflow data
  • Syncing with Atlassian APIs
  • Providing application functionality
  • Troubleshooting and support

4. Types of Personal Data

Personal Data processed may include:

  • Names
  • Usernames
  • Email addresses
  • Profile information
  • Issue content within Atlassian products
  • Comments or checklist data
  • Activity metadata

Processor does not intentionally collect sensitive categories of Personal Data unless included by the Controller in Atlassian issue data.

5. Categories of Data Subjects

Personal Data may relate to:

  • Employees
  • Contractors
  • Collaborators
  • Customers of the Controller
  • Other users of Atlassian products

6. Processor Obligations

Processor shall:

  • Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from Controller
  • Ensure personnel processing Personal Data are bound by confidentiality
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
  • Assist Controller in responding to Data Subject Requests
  • Assist Controller in compliance with GDPR Articles 32–36 where applicable
  • Delete or return Personal Data at the end of the Services
  • Maintain records of processing activities where required

7. Security Measures

Processor shall implement appropriate security measures including:

Access Controls

  • Role-based access control
  • Least privilege principle
  • Authentication mechanisms

Infrastructure Security

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS)
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Vulnerability monitoring

Data Protection

  • Encryption in transit
  • Logical isolation of customer data
  • Secure storage

Operational Security

  • Logging and monitoring
  • Incident response procedures
  • Employee security training

Processor shall regularly review and update these measures.

8. Subprocessors

Controller authorizes Processor to use Subprocessors to provide the Services.

Processor shall:

  • Ensure Subprocessors are bound by data protection obligations equivalent to this DPA
  • Remain responsible for Subprocessor performance

A current list of Subprocessors may include providers for:

  • Cloud hosting
  • Monitoring services
  • Support tools
  • Analytics tools

Processor shall notify Controller of any intended changes concerning Subprocessors.

9. International Data Transfers

Where Personal Data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), Processor shall ensure appropriate safeguards are implemented including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Adequacy decisions
  • Other lawful transfer mechanisms

10. Data Subject Rights

Processor shall assist Controller, where reasonably possible, in fulfilling obligations to respond to Data Subject requests including:

  • Access
  • Rectification
  • Erasure
  • Restriction
  • Portability
  • Objection

Requests received directly by Processor shall be forwarded to Controller unless legally required otherwise.

11. Security Incidents

Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a Security Incident involving Personal Data.

Notification shall include:

  • Description of the incident
  • Likely consequences
  • Measures taken or proposed
  • Steps to mitigate harm

Processor shall cooperate with Controller in investigation and remediation.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Processor shall retain Personal Data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services.

Upon termination of the Services, Processor shall, at Controller's choice:

  • Delete Personal Data, or
  • Return Personal Data to Controller

Backups may be retained temporarily where required for security or legal purposes.

13. Audit Rights

Controller may request reasonable information demonstrating Processor's compliance with this DPA.

Where required by law, Controller may conduct audits or inspections subject to:

  • Reasonable notice
  • Confidentiality obligations
  • Minimal disruption to Processor operations

14. Confidentiality

Processor shall ensure all personnel authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive appropriate data protection training.

15. Liability

Each party's liability under this Agreement shall be subject to the limitations set out in the underlying agreement between the parties unless otherwise required by Applicable Data Protection Laws.

16. Term and Termination

This Agreement remains in effect for the duration of the Services during which Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of Controller.

Upon termination of the Services, the obligations under this DPA regarding data protection and confidentiality shall survive.

17. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the law governing the underlying agreement between Controller and Processor, unless required otherwise by applicable data protection law.

18. Contact Information

For questions regarding this Agreement or data protection matters:

  • Bizappe
  • Email: legal@bizappe.com
